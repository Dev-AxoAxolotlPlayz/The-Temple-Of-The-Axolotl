# 🏛️ Temple of the Axolotl

### ✨ The Cosmic and the Seas

**A celestial adventure awaits beneath the waves.**

Welcome to **Temple of the Axolotl**, a Minecraft mod that brings mysterious temples, powerful celestial artifacts, supernatural companions, and the wonders of the cosmic ocean into your world!

When the lore of friendly, flippered axolotls goes deeper than expected... sometimes there's much more than meets the eye.

---

## 🌊 About the Mod

Temple of the Axolotl is a celestial-themed Minecraft mod developed by **Dev-AxoAxolotlPlayz**, combining exploration, supernatural abilities, underwater environments, and mysterious lore.

Discover the secrets of ancient temples, harness celestial powers, and venture into the depths of the Ethereal Abyss.

But be warned: the deeper you descend, the greater the dangers become.

## ✨ Features

### 🏛️ Celestial Temples

Explore mysterious structures, discover hidden secrets, and uncover the history of the celestial axolotls.

### ⚔️ Celestial Equipment and Artifacts

Discover powerful equipment, custom enchantments, and unique abilities that expand your adventures beyond ordinary Minecraft.

### 🌌 The Ethereal Abyss

Experience a mysterious underwater environment featuring:

- Custom water visuals and atmospheric effects.
- Underwater pressure mechanics.
- Increasing environmental dangers at greater depths.
- Specialized underwater exploration mechanics.

### 🐉 Celestial Companions

Encounter two extraordinary celestial beings:

- **Above All** — A powerful celestial companion with unique abilities.
- **Wrath** — A formidable combat companion.

### 🎨 Custom Celestial Interface

Enjoy a cosmic ocean-themed Minecraft title screen featuring animated underwater scenery and our beloved chicken mascot, **Cluckles!** 🐔

### 🔧 Mod Compatibility

Temple of the Axolotl includes title-screen integration with:

- Essential
- Create

Additional compatibility improvements, including support for Create addons, are planned for future updates.

## 📦 Installation

**Current Release:** v0.1.0 — The Cosmic and the Seas

| Requirement | Version |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | 1.21.1 |
| Mod Loader | NeoForge |
| Java | 21 |

1. Install NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.1.
2. Download the latest Temple of the Axolotl release from the repository's **Releases** section.
3. Install the required dependencies listed below.
4. Place the mod JAR and its dependencies into your Minecraft `mods` folder.
5. Launch Minecraft and enjoy!

## 📚 Required Dependencies

- **Vanilla Backport** — Required for certain crafting recipes and other features.
- **GeckoLib 4** — Required for rendering custom entity models.
- **Platform** — Supporting library used by Vanilla Backport.
- **Patchouli** — Required for the lore and hint book.

Make sure each dependency is compatible with Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge.

## 🧰 Recommended Mods

- **Essential** — Additional multiplayer functionality.
- **Create** — Mechanical automation, building, and crafting.
- **Enchanting Infuser** — Easier access to custom enchantments. Enable treasure enchanting in your world configuration where applicable.

These mods are recommended but are not required for the core experience.
